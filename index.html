How to Download Your Strava History

Official Strava Data Export Flow
1 strava.com

Open Account Settings

Log into Strava.com on your computer. Hover over your profile photo in the top right and click Settings, then click My Account.

2 Archive Request

Request Your Archive

Scroll down to Download or Delete Your Account. Under Step 2, click Get Started, then click Request Your Archive.

3 Instant Analysis

Drop Archive Here

Strava will email a download link in a few minutes. Download your .zip file (e.g. strava_export_*.zip) and drag it into the box below.
